How the AWCA Lottery works

Entries

Each active AWCA Lottery subscription counts as one entry in the draw. Cancelled subscriptions are removed automatically.

Total winnings available

Total winnings available are calculated from live entries and updated automatically by the backend system.

Random selection

A secure randomiser selects a winning entry. Duplicate email addresses are handled fairly using aliases.

Persistent records

Entries are stored in a persistent data store, ensuring accurate totals and reliable draw history.

Community impact

The AWCA Lottery supports community initiatives across Alconbury Weald. Funds raised contribute to local projects, events and awards.
