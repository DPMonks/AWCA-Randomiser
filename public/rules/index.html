AWCA Lottery

Official monthly community draw for Alconbury Weald

AWCA Lottery rules

  1. The lottery is run by Alconbury Weald Community Association, The Club, the Blvd, Alconbury Weald, Huntingdon PE28 4XA.
  2. You must be 16 or over to enter. Nobody under 16 may buy an entry or win a prize. We may ask for proof of age before paying a prize. Any entry from someone under 16 is void and will be refunded.
  3. Each entry costs £2.50 a month through the AWCA Lottery plan. Each active plan is one entry, so you can hold more than one.
  4. Half of every entry (£1.25) goes into the prize pot. The other half supports the Association.
  5. One winner is drawn each month at the date and time shown on the lottery page. The winner is picked at random by computer, and the result can't be changed or redrawn.
  6. Only plans that are active at the time of the draw are entered. If you cancel your plan, your entry stops.
  7. The winner is shown on the lottery page by initials and entry code, and is contacted by email.
  8. Questions or complaints: Community-Association@alconbury-weald.org
